The Trip

In the summer of 1976 the Bond family — Graham, Monique, Denise (12), Francis (9) and Helena (7) — packed a white Ford Transit camper van named Snowy Fred and drove from England to Bombay, through Yugoslavia, Greece, Turkey, Iran, Afghanistan, Pakistan and India, before flying on to Australia via Bangkok and Singapore. This site rebuilds our trip from the diaries, letters and slides.

Mum (Monique) took most of the photos, but we couldn't develop them until we reached Australia! We kids were without school for 5 months, so everyday we would have to write a paragraph in our diary. It was a fantastic trip!
